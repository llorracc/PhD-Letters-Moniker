# Target job postings (student workspace)

Use this as a **scratchpad** while you explore the market: attractive postings, why they fit your work, and who to contact (search chairs, faculty with related research). It is **not** read by `emails-make.sh`; it helps you draft accurate `OPTIONAL_INSTITUTION_FIT` / `OPTIONAL_PERSON_FIT` lines in each `recipients/<org>/email@domain.md` file.

## How to use

- Add a section per institution or posting.
- Use checklists for people you might email.
- Paste links to job ads or department pages.
- When you are ready to generate mail, create or edit the corresponding file under `recipients/<organization>/`.
