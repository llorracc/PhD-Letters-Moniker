# Recipient Information Template

This is a template for creating recipient files. Copy this structure and fill in the information.

**IMPORTANT CONVENTION:**
- **UPPERCASE fields** = Content that appears in the email
- **lowercase fields** = Private notes to advisor (NOT inserted in email)

## Required Fields (Appear in Email)

PERSONAL_NAME: FirstName
FAMILY_NAME: LastName

## Optional Fields

NICKNAME:  # Appears in email salutation (e.g., "Dear Alex" instead of "Dear Alexander")
optional_private_comment_to_advisor:  # Private note/question for advisor only (e.g., "I think you told me this person was a colleague of yours at the Fed?")

## Email Content (Optional - All INSERTED IN EMAIL)

OPTIONAL_INSTITUTION_FIT: Alex's work on wealth inequality and heterogeneous-agent models would be a strong fit for [Institution]'s macroeconomics group.

OPTIONAL_PERSON_FIT: I know your longstanding interest in the distribution of wealth, so I think you'll be particularly interested in Alex's research.

CUSTOM_CONTENT: Alex tells me you were a mentor of his in the ASSA program for URMs, and that your advice was very helpful to him.
